free workshops · north texas libraries

Prompted runs free, hands-on AI workshops at public libraries across North Texas. One hour. No experience needed. Leave actually knowing how to use it.

Find a workshop Volunteer
mission

AI is changing everything.
Most people were never shown how to use it.

Prompted exists to close that gap. We bring free, practical AI education to the places everyone already trusts — public libraries — so that age, income, and background never decide who gets to keep up.

how it works

Three steps. That's it.

No sign-up fees, no homework, no jargon. Just show up.

01

Find a workshop

Browse upcoming sessions at libraries near you and register in two clicks. Every workshop is completely free.

02

Show up

Bring yourself and any device — or use a library computer. A volunteer sits with you in a small group the whole time.

03

Learn to prompt AI

Work through real projects, hands-on. Learn how to ask better questions, spot AI mistakes, and use it safely at home.
