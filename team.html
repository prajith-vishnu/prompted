the team

Meet the team.

Prompted is built and run by volunteers who believe AI literacy should belong to everyone. Here's who's behind it — and how you can be part of it.

founder

Hi — I'm Prajith.

I started Prompted as a high school student in Denton, Texas, after noticing the same thing again and again: the people who stood to gain the most from AI were the ones no one was teaching.

Friends my age picked these tools up in an afternoon. But adults I respected — people who were sharp at everything they did — kept telling me they felt left behind, didn't trust it, or just didn't know where to start. That gap didn't sit right with me.

So I built Prompted around one simple idea: meet people where they already are — the public library — and sit down next to them while they actually try it. No lectures, no jargon, no cost.

This is just the beginning, and I'd love for you to be part of it — as a participant, a volunteer, or a library that wants to host us.

> — Prajith Vishnu, founder_

join us

The team is growing.

Right now Prompted is small and volunteer-run — which means there's real room to shape what it becomes. If you care about getting AI into more hands, there's a place for you here. No AI expertise required.

Workshop volunteers

Sit with two or three participants and guide them through hands-on AI projects. We prep you before every session — you're never thrown in unprepared.

Library partners

Work at a North Texas library, or know one that would host us? Help us bring a free workshop to your community.

Spread the word

Tell a friend, a parent, a neighbor. The people who need this most are often the ones who'd never sign up on their own.
