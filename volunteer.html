volunteer

Teach someone
something that matters.

You don't need to be an AI expert or a teacher. You need patience, curiosity, and one Saturday hour a month.

why volunteer

The best way to learn AI
is to teach it.

Real impact, fast

In one hour you'll watch someone go from "I don't get it" to building something themselves. That moment never gets old.

Skills that compound

Explaining AI to a beginner sharpens your own understanding more than any tutorial. Plus: leadership and communication experience that's actually real.

A team worth joining

Work alongside other students and community members who care about the same thing. Service hours available for students.

what volunteers do

Sit with 2–3 people.
Guide them through real projects.

During each workshop you're paired with a small group of participants. You help them get set up, walk them through hands-on AI projects, encourage experimentation, and answer questions as they go. We give you a full volunteer guide and a walkthrough before every session — you'll never be thrown in unprepared. No teaching experience needed.
